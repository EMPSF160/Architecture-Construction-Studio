Foundational Grammar

Core Design Principles

The four invariant tenets that govern every sketch, section, and physical model created in our studio.

01

CONTEXT

Architecture begins with place. We study regional topography, geological formations, native stone quarries, and bioclimatic wind patterns.

02

PROPORTION

Space is defined through relationships. Harmonious ratios between compression and release, column intervals, and ceiling datums dictate emotional comfort.

03

LIGHT

Light gives architecture time. Deep reveals, clerestories, and courtyards register the passing hours and seasonal sun shifts upon textured surfaces.

04

MATERIAL

Material creates atmosphere. Raw limestone, board-formed slag concrete, and seasoned timber impart tactile warmth and generational durability.
