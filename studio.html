DISCOVER OUR PRAXIS
The Manifesto

Architecture must withstand the erosion of time and trends through genuine physical substance.

We was founded on a singular conviction: the contemporary division between the architect who dreams on screens and the contractor who executes on site is detrimental to architectural culture. When the designer does not feel the weight of stone or understand the curing chemistry of pozzolanic concrete, buildings lose their tectonic soul.

By unifying architectural research, computational structural engineering, and self-performed craftsmanship under one roof, our studio ensures that every line drawn on paper is realized with millimeter precision on the construction site. We embrace raw local aggregates, unadorned joinery, and passive thermodynamic principles.

01

Material Integrity

We refuse synthetic simulations. If a wall appears to be stone, it is load-bearing stone. If a beam is timber, it is solid seasoned teak or engineered glulam.

02

Contextual Vernacular

Every building emerges directly from its geological, climatic, and cultural terrain, utilizing passive courtyard cooling and natural solar orientations.

03

Craft as Praxis

Our construction division crafts 1:1 physical joinery mockups before site pours, cultivating close dialogue with master stonemasons and ironworkers.

Design & Build Methodology

Studio Sequence

From initial site observation to the final tactile refinement of hand-waxed surfaces, our workflow is rigorous and uninterrupted.

01

Observe

Analyzing microclimates, geological strata, sun paths, wind vectors, and the cultural memory of the terrain.

02

Draw

Expressing spatial ideas through hand charcoal sketches, proportion studies, and axonometric geometry.

03

Model

Constructing physical timber, plaster, and bronze study models to test light penetration and volumetric mass.

04

Detail

Engineering shadow gaps, thermal breaks, concealed drainage channels, and tectonic junction tolerances.

05

Build

Directing site operations with our dedicated construction team, ensuring strict material standards.

06

Refine

Hand-burnishing lime washes, testing acoustic damping, and observing how the structure settles into place.
