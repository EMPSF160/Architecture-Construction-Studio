The Tectonic Chain

Design → Engineering → Material → Craft → Site → Completion

Our construction division operates not as a speculative commercial contractor, but as a dedicated spatial workshop. We maintain our own carpentry shops, stone-dressing yards, and structural rigging specialists.

This direct integration allows us to make real-time refinements on site, responding to the nuance of an exposed aggregate or adjusting a stone quoin to catch the afternoon sun.

Sequential Execution

The 6 Stages of Construction

Stage 01

01 — Planning & Prototyping

Constructing 1:1 joinery mockups, slump test samples, and scheduling non-destructive ultrasonic tests.

Stage 02

02 — Groundwork & Bedrock

Excavating into native granite strata, integrating subterranean cisterns, and pouring monolithic grade beams.

Stage 03

03 — Structure & Pour

Erecting post-tensioned columns, assembling board-formed timber shuttering, and continuous self-consolidating pours.

Stage 04

04 — Envelope & Skin

Laying hand-dressed limestone masonry cavities, installing structural low-iron glass fins, and sealing zinc roofs.

Stage 05

05 — Interiors & Craft

Applying multiple coats of natural lime plaster, fitting mortise-tenon teak louvers, and laying basalt pavers.

Stage 06

06 — Completion & Settling

Fine calibration of passive airflow dampers, commissioning lighting sequences, and hand-waxing timber surfaces.

Joinery & Tectonics

Architectural Detail Interfaces

Precision drawings and detail sections demonstrating how disparate materials resolve cleanly at millimeter scales.

Detail 01

Structural Glazing Pocket

Concealed stainless steel channel embedded directly into cast concrete ceiling slabs, eliminating external perimeter frames.

Tolerance: < 1.5mm / 6m Span
Detail 02

Masonry Shadow Reveal

A 25mm recessed zinc reglet between load-bearing limestone piers and ceiling formwork, allowing micro-expansion and sharp shadow lines.

Thermal Break: EPDM Core
Detail 03

Timber Pin Connection

Machined bronze knurled dowels securing triple-ply timber roof trusses to reinforced shear walls without visible weld seams.

Yield Strength: 420 MPa
